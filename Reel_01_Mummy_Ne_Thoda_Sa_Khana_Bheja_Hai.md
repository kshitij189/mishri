# Mishri — Reel 01 Production Pack

## मम्मी ने थोड़ा सा खाना भेजा है

**Duration:** 96 seconds, comprising 12 clips of 8 seconds each.  
**Spoken language:** Natural conversational Hindi.  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16.  
**Setting:** An ordinary residential neighbourhood in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Use a real Jaipur neighbourhood reference for exterior appearance. The home, residents, delivery and incident are fictional.  
**Tone:** Everyday warmth, gentle humour, friendship and a believable last-minute problem.

Mishri has collected a navy-blue food bag from Aditi’s mother elsewhere in Jaipur. She delivers it to Aditi’s rented flat. Aditi invites her to share breakfast. During the meal, a phone call reveals that the mother handed over the bag intended for Aditi’s father’s train journey.

This is the first reel’s combined writing and generation reference. It contains both character portrait prompts, the complete script, and Google Flow instructions for all twelve clips. It does not contain generated portraits or videos.

## Rules for this series and future files

- Mishri remains the main character. Preserve her approved face, fur pattern, outfit, personality and occupation.
- Real Indian destinations and relatable daily life remain central. New destinations are not limited to any previously mentioned cities.
- Familiar supporting characters can return. New locals and customers can appear when a story needs them.
- Stories include ordinary work, money, meals, friends, family, small joys and mistakes. Suspense should follow naturally from these situations.
- Every generated clip is eight seconds. Each reel uses as many clips as its story needs; two minutes is not a required length.
- Create one separate Markdown production pack per reel, using names such as `Reel_02_<Title>.md`.
- This first pack contains Mishri’s master portrait prompt. Future packs should say to reuse her approved reference instead of repeating it.
- Aditi’s portrait prompt is also established here. Reuse her approved reference whenever she returns.
- Future packs include portrait prompts for newly introduced visible characters, plus voice instructions for new voice-only roles.
